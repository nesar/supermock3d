# Red and blue galaxies come from the stars, not from dust

<div class="small tight">

- In production every galaxy at the same redshift has the same dust. Thus the two peaks in $g-i$ are made by the star-formation histories: galaxies with log sSFR < −11 (quiescent) form the red peak, the others the blue one.
- The quiescent fraction falls from 37% at $z$ < 0.3 to 11% at $z$ > 1.2 in the calibration library (all galaxies with log M$_*$ > 0, weighted to the patch).

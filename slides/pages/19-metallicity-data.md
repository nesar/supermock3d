# Where the metallicity relation comes from

- The relation is a straight-line fit in log M$_*$ and $z$ to the stellar mass–metallicity relation of the **EAGLE hydrodynamic simulation** (De Rossi et al. 2017, Fig. 5, digitized), at $z$ = 0, 0.7 and 3.
- It is not fitted to observations. The EAGLE values are close to observed relations at $z$ = 0 (Gallazzi et al. 2005 within the error bars), but this is a known weak point of the model.
- One fit, no scatter, clipped to [−2, 0.3] (the range of the C3K library). The same value is used for the stars and for the gas.
- Data file and provenance: `Mocks_v3/pipeline/data/metallicity/`.

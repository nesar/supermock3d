# Optical colours against SDSS

![SDSS corner](figs/val_corner_sdss.png)

|||

- Mock: a random draw inside 13.4 < $i$ < 20.3 and $z$ < 0.42, with the SDSS filters and the SDSS errors added. SDSS: its own spectroscopic selection (brighter, lower $z$). Thus the $i$ and $z$ histograms differ by design. The mock is not reweighted.
- $r-i$ and $i-z$ agree: medians 0.42 / 0.33 against 0.45 / 0.33.
- **$u-g$ is too blue**: median 1.0 against 1.8.
- **$g-i$ is too red, with too many red galaxies**: median 1.42 against 1.00; 46% against 24% above $g-i$ = 1.5.
- Part of the $g-i$ difference is the redshift mismatch (the mock sample is at higher $z$). The $u-g$ offset was present in the earlier mocks too. Its cause is not known: candidates are the ultraviolet light of old populations, residual star formation in quenched UM histories, and the dust curve.

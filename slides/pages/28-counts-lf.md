# Number counts and luminosity function

<div class="small">

- $r$-band counts agree with the Blanton+03 prediction to **0.070 dex** (mean |Δ|, 15 < $r$ < 19.5, 19 patches). This range is the count term of the dust calibration (fitted on patch 4), so it is a consistency check across patches, not an independent test. Fainter than $r \approx 19$ the mock lies above the curve because the prediction stops at $z$ = 0.55.
- Rest-frame $M_r$ LF at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.2: within 0.9–1.2 of Blanton+03 for $M_r$ = −20 to −23; ~6× low at $M_r$ = −18 (core mass floor).

# Borrowing star-formation histories from UniverseMachine

<div class="small">

- Reference: UniverseMachine DR1 best fit (Behroozi et al. 2019) on SMDPL (400 $h^{-1}$Mpc, 3840³ particles), halos with $M_{\rm peak}$ > 10¹¹. Each galaxy has `sfr_history_all_prog` on 117 snapshots and a surviving stellar mass `sm`.
- Three pools by core class: **centrals** → UM centrals at $a$ = 1; **satellites** → UM non-centrals at $a$ = 1; **merged cores** → UM non-centrals at $a$ = 1 or at $a$ = 0.6643, split by lightcone-crossing epoch.
- Match: nearest neighbour in $[t_{50}, t_{25}, \log M_{\rm peak}, t_{\rm infall}]$ (Gyr, Gyr, M$_\odot$, Gyr), each feature z-scored with its pool's mean and standard deviation. The core inherits the UM galaxy's SFH and $a$ = 1 stellar mass.

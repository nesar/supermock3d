# Star-formation histories come from UniverseMachine

<div class="small">

- **Source.** UniverseMachine DR1 best fit (Behroozi et al. 2019) on the SMDPL simulation (400 $h^{-1}$Mpc, 3840³ particles), halos with $M_{\rm peak}$ > 10¹¹ M$_\odot$. Each UM galaxy has a star-formation rate at 117 times (all progenitors included) and a stellar mass today.
- **Three pools, one per core class.** Centrals → UM centrals today. Satellites → UM non-centrals today. Merged cores → UM non-centrals today or at $a$ = 0.664, by the time the core crossed the lightcone.
- **Match.** Nearest neighbour in $[t_{50}, t_{25}, \log M_{\rm peak}, t_{\rm infall}]$. Each feature is scaled by the mean and standard deviation of its pool, so all four count equally. The core receives the SFH and the stellar mass of its UM neighbour.

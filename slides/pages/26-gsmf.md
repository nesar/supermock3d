# Stellar-mass function

<div class="small tight">

- Between log M$_*$ = 9.8 and 11.3 the mock is within a factor 0.9 to 1.5 of Baldry et al. (2012). **Below 9.6** it falls off. This is the core mass floor (80 particles), not a property of the galaxy model.
- **Above 11.4** the mock is above GAMA (1.5× at 11.3, 6× at 11.5). The mock masses have no measurement scatter, so this is not Eddington bias in the mock.
- At log M$_*$ = 10.5 the number density falls about 20× from $z \approx 0.2$ to $z \approx 2.6$. The patch-to-patch scatter at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.3 is 5 to 10% below log M$_*$ = 11.

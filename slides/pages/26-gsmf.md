# Stellar mass function

<div class="small tight">

- Between log M$_*$ = 9.8 and 11.3 the mock is within a factor 0.9–1.5 of Baldry+12. **Below 9.6** it falls off: the core seeding floor (80 particles), not a property of the galaxy model.
- **Above 11.4** the mock exceeds GAMA (×1.5 at 11.3, ×6 at 11.5). The mock masses carry no measurement scatter, so this is not Eddington bias in the mock.
- At log M$_*$ = 10.5 the number density falls by ~20× from $z \approx 0.2$ to $z \approx 2.6$. Patch-to-patch scatter at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.3 is 5–10% below log M$_*$ = 11.

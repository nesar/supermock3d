# Optical colours vs DEEP2

![DEEP2 corner](figs/val_corner_deep2.png)

|||

- Mock: 18.1 < $i$ < 23.6, $z$ < 1.05, DEEP2 per-object errors added.
- $r-i$ and $i-z$ agree well (0.62 / 0.38 vs 0.61 / 0.36), including their trends with redshift.
- The same pattern as in SDSS: **$u-g$ bluer** (0.32 vs 0.73) and **$g-i$ redder** (1.31 vs 0.80, 38% vs 16% above 1.5).
- The $i$ distribution matches for $i$ < 22.5; DEEP2's targeting cuts at $i \approx 23$ and pre-selects $z$ > 0.7, which is visible in its redshift marginal.

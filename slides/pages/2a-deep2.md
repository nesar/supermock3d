# Optical colours against DEEP2

![DEEP2 corner](figs/val_corner_deep2.png)

|||

- Mock: 18.1 < $i$ < 23.6, $z$ < 1.05, DEEP2 errors added.
- $r-i$ and $i-z$ agree well (0.62 / 0.38 against 0.61 / 0.36), including their change with redshift.
- The same pattern as for SDSS: **$u-g$ bluer** (0.32 against 0.73) and **$g-i$ redder** (1.31 against 0.80; 38% against 16% above 1.5).
- The $i$ histograms agree for $i$ < 22.5. DEEP2 targets stop at $i \approx 23$ and prefer $z$ > 0.7, which shows in its redshift histogram.

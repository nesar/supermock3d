# What the fit sees, 1: optical colours

<div class="small tight">

- Made like the fit itself: the dust-free spectra of the patch-4 calibration library (100,000 of 256,000 galaxies shown) are re-attenuated with each dust model and projected through the SDSS filters. The survey's own errors are added, and only galaxies inside the survey's redshift and $i$-band window are kept.
- Dust moves $r-i$ and $i-z$ onto the data. $u-g$ stays too blue and $g-i$ keeps a stronger red peak than the data: these are the two colour offsets reported in Part 2.

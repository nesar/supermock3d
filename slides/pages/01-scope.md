# What the mock is for, and what it is

<div class="small tight">

- **Purpose:** galaxies with realistic SEDs and colours on a lightcone, to test the SPHEREx redshift and cosmology pipelines (and the LSST, WISE, COSMOS, Legacy Surveys, 2MASS photometry they use) on known truth.
- **Construction:** cores from a 3.4 $h^{-1}$Gpc N-body run, UniverseMachine star-formation histories, stellar-population synthesis, explicit dust and IGM.
- **Not a fit to SPHEREx data:** only the dust model is tuned, on SDSS, DEEP2, WISE×SuperCOSMOS colours and $r$-band counts.

</div>

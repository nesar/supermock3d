# Near-IR SED shapes vs GAMA stacks

![GAMA stacks](figs/val_gama.png)

Caption: GAMA median observed-frame stacks (solid) and mock medians selected the same way ($|z-z_0|$ < 0.015, $r$ < 19.8, mass bins in $M_*(z_{\rm obs})$; dashed), 19 patches. Top: absolute flux. Bottom: normalised at $1.25(1+z)$ µm. The 1.6 µm bump, the 2.3 µm CO break and Paα agree in position and contrast; redward of ~2.3 µm rest the mock continuum is higher than GAMA's. **At fixed mass label the mock is 1.3–2.8× fainter** (median 1.8×), consistent with a 0.2–0.3 dex offset between UM stellar masses and GAMA's SED-fit masses.

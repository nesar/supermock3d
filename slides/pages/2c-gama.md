# Near-infrared spectrum shapes against GAMA stacks

![GAMA stacks](figs/val_gama.png)

Caption: GAMA median observed-frame stacks (solid) and mock medians selected the same way (|$z-z_0$| < 0.015, $r$ < 19.8, mass bins in $M_*(z_{\rm obs})$; dashed), 19 patches. Top: absolute flux. Bottom: normalised at 1.25(1+$z$) µm. The 1.6 µm bump, the 2.3 µm CO break and the Paschen-α line agree in position and contrast. Beyond about 2.3 µm rest the mock continuum is higher than GAMA. **At a fixed mass label the mock is 1.3 to 2.8× fainter** (median 1.8×). This agrees with a 0.2 to 0.3 dex offset between UM stellar masses and the SED-fit masses of GAMA.

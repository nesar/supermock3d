# The three pools, and the cores that query them

<div class="small tight">

- The pools come from 5 of the 24 SMDPL subvolumes: 25,043 centrals and 9,175 non-centrals at $a$ = 1, and 9,563 non-centrals at $a$ = 0.664 (the last for merged cores that crossed the lightcone before $a$ = 0.664, so their history ends there).
- The pools start at $M_{\rm peak}$ = 10¹¹ M$_\odot$, the cores at 3.2 × 10¹¹. Lightcone centrals grow later than the UM centrals ($t_{50}$ about 5 Gyr against 2 to 4 Gyr); satellites and merged cores grow early and have an infall time, as the non-central pools do.

# Mid-infrared colours against WISE

![WISE corner](figs/val_corner_wise.png)

|||

- **W1−W2** (starlight, 3.4 and 4.6 µm): mock median 0.35, WISE×SuperCOSMOS 0.37 (Vega). The CO absorption in the C3K library brings the old populations to this value.
- **W2−W3** (warm dust): mock 2.56 against 3.63. This is a selection difference, not a dust error. The WISE×SuperCOSMOS sample needs W3 and W4 detections, so it holds mostly star-forming galaxies. The mock sample also holds quiescent galaxies (which are blue in W2−W3). With the same W3 cut the mock median is 3.57. The all-sky AllWISE galaxy sample, which has no such cut, gives 2.60.
- **W3−W4**: mock 1.65, bluer than both WISE samples (2.15, 2.56). The template ties give a narrow range of dust temperatures, and no active nucleus is painted.
- W3−W4 was not used in the calibration. It is a pure prediction.

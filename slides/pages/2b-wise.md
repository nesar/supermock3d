# Mid-infrared colours vs WISE

![WISE corner](figs/val_corner_wise.png)

|||

- **W1−W2** (stellar, 3.4/4.6 µm): mock median 0.35, WISE×SCOS 0.37 Vega. C3K's CO absorption is what brings the old populations to this value.
- **W2−W3** (PAH and warm dust): mock 2.56 vs 3.63 for WISE×SCOS. That sample requires W3 and W4 detections and so selects star-forming galaxies; the AllWISE galaxy locus, which does not, has 2.60.
- **W3−W4**: mock 1.65, bluer than both WISE samples (2.15, 2.56). The DL07 ties at fixed $(Z,{\rm sSFR})$ give a narrow range of dust temperatures, and no AGN torus is painted.
- W3−W4 was excluded from the calibration; it is a pure prediction.

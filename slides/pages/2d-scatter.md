# Patch-to-patch variation

![patch scatter](figs/val_patch_scatter.png)

Caption: Left: rms of the galaxy count per patch (all galaxies, Δ$z$ = 0.25) over the 19 patches, compared with Poisson noise. Right: each patch's stellar-mass function at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.3 divided by the mean. The rms of 1 to 5% in $N(z)$, against below 0.3% for Poisson, is sample variance of 215 deg² patches. The 19 patches are not independent (most share an edge), so this is a lower limit for separate fields.

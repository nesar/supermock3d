# Patch-to-patch variation

![patch scatter](figs/val_patch_scatter.png)

Caption: Left: rms of the galaxy count per patch (all galaxies, Δ$z$ = 0.25) over the 19 patches, compared with Poisson. Right: each patch's GSMF at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.3 divided by the mean. With 1–5% rms in $N(z)$ against < 0.3% Poisson, the scatter is sample variance of 215 deg² patches; the 19 patches are not independent (most of them share edges), so this is a lower bound for disjoint fields.

# Dust attenuation: explicit, in the rest frame

<div class="small">

- FSPS runs **dust-free**; attenuation is applied afterwards to the rest-frame spectrum: $L^{\rm att}_\lambda = L_\lambda\, e^{-\tau(\lambda)}$, $\tau(\lambda) = \tau_V\,[k(\lambda)/k_V + (E_b/4.05)D(\lambda)]\,(\lambda/0.55\,\mu{\rm m})^{n}$ (Noll et al. 2009; bump tied to slope, $E_b = 0.85-1.9n$, Kriek & Conroy 2013) plus a Charlot & Fall birth-cloud term $\mu\tau_V(\lambda/5500)^{-1}$.
- Population model: $\tau_V = a\,10^{b(\log M_*-10)}\,10^{c\log Z}\,(1+z)^d\,10^{e(\log{\rm sSFR}+10)}$, no scatter. Production fit: $a$ = 0.407, $d$ = 0.584; $b, c, e, n, \mu$ ≈ 0, i.e. a Calzetti curve with a bump and $\tau_V = 0.41(1+z)^{0.58}$.

# One galaxy through the painter

![SED chain](figs/sed_chain_rest.png)

Caption: The production chain re-run on one matched SFH from the patch-3 catalog ($z$ = 1.03, star-forming). Stars only (grey); with nebular continuum and lines (light blue); attenuated with $\tau_V$ = 0.61 (orange); plus the DL07 re-emission of the absorbed 58% of $L_{\rm bol}$ (dark). The far-IR lines in the attenuated spectrum are nebular fine-structure lines.

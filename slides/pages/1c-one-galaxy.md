# One galaxy through the painter

![SED chain](figs/sed_chain_rest.png)

Caption: The production chain run again on one matched history from the patch-3 catalog ($z$ = 1.03, star-forming). Stars only (grey). With gas continuum and lines (light blue). After dust absorption with $\tau_V$ = 0.61 (orange). With the infrared re-emission of the absorbed energy, 58% of $L_{\rm bol}$ (dark). The narrow lines beyond 10 µm in the orange curve are gas lines on a very faint stellar continuum.

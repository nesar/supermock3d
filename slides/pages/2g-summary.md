# Summary

- **Construction:** LastJourney cores on the lightcone → assembly features → UniverseMachine SFHs by standardised nearest-neighbour matching → FSPS-C3K spectra with nebular emission, rest-frame dust and energy-balanced DL07 re-emission, IGM → 159-band AB photometry and rest-frame luminosities. The dust model is the only calibrated component.
- **Scale:** 19 patches, 4,082 deg², 683 M galaxies to $z$ = 5.5; ~36 M galaxies and ~4 h of 8–16 nodes per additional patch.
- **What agrees:** bright $r$-band counts (0.07 dex), the LF from $M_r$ = −20 to −23, the GSMF from log M$_*$ = 9.8 to 11.3 (factor 0.9–1.5), SMHM above 10¹²·⁵ M$_\odot$, $r-i$ and $i-z$ colours, W1−W2, and the near-IR SED shape (1.6 µm bump, CO, Paα).
- **What does not:** $u-g$ and $g-i$, W3−W4, the massive end of the GSMF, everything below the core mass floor, and the absolute flux at fixed mass label against GAMA.
- **Next:** decide the merged-core treatment and recalibrate the dust together with it; diagnose the $z \approx 2.86$ step; dust scatter and an AGN component; paint further patches (band 2 and 3 are partly done).

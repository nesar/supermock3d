# Summary

- **How it is built.** LastJourney cores on the lightcone → four growth features → UniverseMachine star-formation histories by scaled nearest-neighbour match → FSPS spectra with the C3K library, gas emission, rest-frame dust, infrared re-emission by energy balance and intergalactic absorption → 159-band AB photometry and rest-frame luminosities. The dust model is the only calibrated part.
- **Scale.** 19 patches, 4,082 deg², 683 M galaxies to $z$ = 5.5. One more patch is about 36 M galaxies and 4 to 6 hours on 8 to 32 nodes.
- **What agrees.** Bright $r$-band counts (0.07 dex), the luminosity function from $M_r$ = −20 to −23, the stellar-mass function from log M$_*$ = 9.8 to 11.3 (factor 0.9 to 1.5), the stellar-to-halo mass relation above 10¹²·⁵ M$_\odot$, the $r-i$ and $i-z$ colours, W1−W2, and the near-infrared spectrum shape (1.6 µm bump, CO, Paschen-α).
- **What does not.** $u-g$ and $g-i$, W3−W4, the massive end of the stellar-mass function, everything below the core mass floor, and the absolute flux at a fixed mass label against GAMA.

# Stellar-to-halo mass and star formation

- **SMHM** (centrals, $z$ < 0.3): the median follows Moster+13 within 0.1 dex for $M_{\rm peak}$ = 10¹²·⁵–10¹⁴ M$_\odot$. At the floor ($M_{\rm peak}$ < 10¹²) it is 0.3–0.9 dex low: the lowest-mass cores are matched to the sparse low-mass end of the UM pool.
- **sSFR–M$_*$**: a star-forming sequence near log sSFR ≈ −9.8 at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.5 (≈ −9 at 1 < $z$ < 2) and a quenched population at −11 to −12.5, both inherited from UniverseMachine.
- The quenched population is much weaker at 1 < $z$ < 2. Both features come from UniverseMachine; nothing in the painting changes them.

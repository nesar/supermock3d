# Stellar mass against halo mass, and star formation

- **Stellar-to-halo mass** (centrals, $z$ < 0.3): the median follows Moster et al. (2013) within 0.1 dex for $M_{\rm peak}$ = 10¹²·⁵ to 10¹⁴ M$_\odot$. At the floor ($M_{\rm peak}$ < 10¹²) it is 0.3 to 0.9 dex low: the lowest-mass cores are matched to the sparse low-mass end of the UM pool.
- **Star formation against mass**: a star-forming sequence near log sSFR ≈ −9.8 at 0.05 < $z$ < 0.5 (about −9 at 1 < $z$ < 2), and a quenched population at −11 to −12.5. The quenched population is much weaker at 1 < $z$ < 2.
- Both come from UniverseMachine. Nothing in the painting changes them.

# Who is detectable, and where

- Fractions are relative to **all** catalog galaxies in each redshift bin, from 1.2 M random photometry rows per patch (weights restore the full population).
- A SPHEREx channel at 19.5 AB (an approximate all-sky depth used throughout the reports) sees ~40% of galaxies at $z \approx 0.4$, 4% at $z \approx 1.1$ and < 1% beyond $z \approx 2$.
- LSST $i$ < 25.3 keeps > 50% of all galaxies to $z \approx 1.4$. Its mock surface density, 19.5 arcmin⁻², is about half of the ~40 arcmin⁻² usually projected for LSST: at that depth the mock is limited by the core mass floor, not by flux.
- The mock contains only galaxies: no stars, quasars or blends.

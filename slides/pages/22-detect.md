# Which galaxies each survey can see

- The fractions are relative to **all** catalog galaxies in each redshift bin. They come from 1.2 M random photometry rows per patch, with weights that restore the full population.
- A SPHEREx channel at 19.5 AB (the all-sky depth used in the reports) sees about 40% of galaxies at $z \approx 0.4$, 4% at $z \approx 1.1$ and below 1% beyond $z \approx 2$.
- LSST $i$ < 25.3 keeps more than 50% of all galaxies to $z \approx 1.4$. Its surface density in the mock, 19.5 per arcmin², is about half of the 40 per arcmin² expected for LSST. At that depth the mock is limited by the core mass floor, not by flux.
- The mock contains only galaxies: no stars, quasars or blends.

# Colour against redshift

![colours vs z](figs/val_colors_z.png)

Caption: LSST colours of the cut sample against observed redshift (19 patches). The ridges are the 4000 Å / Balmer break crossing the band edges: $g-r$ peaks at $z \approx 0.4$, $r-i$ at $z \approx 0.75$, $i-z$ at $z \approx 1.1$. The red sequence sits above the star-forming galaxies. The thin diagonal tracks are single bright emission lines crossing a filter. A photo-$z$ code trained on the mock learns these relations.

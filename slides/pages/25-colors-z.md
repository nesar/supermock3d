# Colour–redshift relations

![colours vs z](figs/val_colors_z.png)

Caption: LSST colours of the cut sample against observed redshift (19 patches, number-weighted). Ridges are the 4000 Å / Balmer break crossing band edges (e.g. $g-r$ peaks at $z \approx 0.4$, $r-i$ at $z \approx 0.75$, $i-z$ at $z \approx 1.1$), with the red sequence above the star-forming locus. Thin diagonal tracks are individual bright emission lines crossing filters. These relations are what a photo-$z$ code trained on the mock will learn.

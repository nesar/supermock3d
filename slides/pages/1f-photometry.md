# Photometry and rest-frame luminosities

<div class="small">

- AB magnitudes with the photon-counting definition $\langle f_\nu\rangle = \int f_\nu T\,d\lambda/\lambda \big/ \int T\,d\lambda/\lambda$, $m = -2.5\log_{10}(\langle f_\nu\rangle/3631\,{\rm Jy})$. All bands of all galaxies are one matrix product on a fixed $R$ = 8000 grid (< 1.5 mmag from brute-force quadrature).
- Rest-frame luminosities without K-corrections: $L_\nu = 4\pi D_L^2 f_\nu/(1+z)$ on the rest grid, projected through SDSS and WISE filters; $L_{\rm bol}$ and $L_{8-33\,\mu{\rm m}}$ (the integral stops at 33 µm rest, so it is not the total 8–1000 µm $L_{\rm IR}$).

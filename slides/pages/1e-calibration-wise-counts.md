# What the fit sees, 2: WISE colours and bright-galaxy counts

<div class="small tight">

- **WISE** (left, middle): without dust there is no infrared emission and W2−W3 sits near 1. The energy balance moves it to 2 to 3. The WISE sample is redder (3.6) because it needs W3 and W4 detections, which select star-forming galaxies.
- **Counts** (right): the first fit, which looked only at colours, put so much dust on massive galaxies that the mock has 2 to 5× too few galaxies at $r$ < 18 and almost none brighter than $r$ = 16. The production model follows the Blanton et al. (2003) prediction over the whole fitted range. (The bright end is noisy here because only 100,000 of the 256,000 library galaxies are used.)

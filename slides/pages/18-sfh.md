# From the matched SFH to a galaxy at $z_{\rm obs}$

<div class="small">

- The galaxy observed at $z_{\rm obs}$ is the matched UM galaxy's history **truncated at the cosmic time $t(z_{\rm obs})$**: only stars formed before then are painted.
- Stellar mass at the epoch of observation: $M_*(z_{\rm obs}) = f_{\rm surv}\int_0^{t(z_{\rm obs})}{\rm SFR}\,dt$, with $f_{\rm surv}$ = 0.572 measured from the pool itself (median of UM `sm` over the formed mass; identical in all 19 patches). The painted spectrum uses FSPS's own surviving mass.
- The catalog keeps both `stellar_mass` (UM, $a$ = 1, valid at low $z$ only) and `stellar_mass_zobs`.

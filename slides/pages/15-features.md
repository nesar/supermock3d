# Four numbers describe how each core grew

From the core's **own** mass history $M(t)$ over the 101 outputs:

- $M_{\rm peak}$: the largest value of $M(t)$
- $t_{50}$, $t_{25}$: the first time $M(t)$ reaches 50% and 25% of $M_{\rm peak}$
- $t_{\rm infall}$: the first output at which the core is no longer a central (today, if never)

We convert output numbers to cosmic time with $a = 1/201 + (1-1/201)(s+1)/500$, and masses from $h^{-1}$M$_\odot$ to M$_\odot$.

The history runs to $z$ = 0 even for a core observed at high redshift (checked: every core at $z$ > 2 has a mass at the final output). The four numbers describe the whole growth. The time of observation enters later, when we cut the star-formation history.

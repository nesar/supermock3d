# Four numbers per core summarise its assembly

From the core's **own** infall-tree-node mass history $M(t)$ over the 101 outputs:

- $M_{\rm peak}$: maximum of $M(t)$
- $t_{50}$, $t_{25}$: first time $M(t)$ reaches 50% and 25% of $M_{\rm peak}$
- $t_{\rm infall}$: first output at which the core is no longer a central (present day if never)

Steps are converted to cosmic time with $a = 1/201 + (1-1/201)(s+1)/500$; masses from $h^{-1}$M$_\odot$ to M$_\odot$.

The history runs to $z$ = 0 even for cores observed at high redshift (verified: 100% of $z$ > 2 cores have a non-zero final-output mass). The features describe the whole assembly; the time of observation enters later, when the SFH is truncated.
